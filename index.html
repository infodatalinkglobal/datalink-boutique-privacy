Privacy Policy

Datalink Boutique Demo · Last updated: 20 October 2026

Datalink Boutique Demo is a small test project that shows how a business can answer customers on WhatsApp. It is run by Sharifdeen Tahiru in Accra, Ghana. This page explains what information the project handles and what happens to it. It is written in plain language on purpose.

What information we handle

When someone sends a WhatsApp message to the project's number, we receive:

  • the phone number the message came from, and the WhatsApp profile name if WhatsApp provides it;
  • the content of the message (text, and the details of any media or location sent);
  • the time the message was sent, and delivery and read status for replies we send back.

We do not ask for passwords, payment card details or identity documents, and we ask people not to send them.

Why we handle it

  • To read and reply to messages sent to the project's WhatsApp number.
  • To keep a record of each conversation so it can be continued and reviewed while we test and improve the service.

We do not sell this information and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored and who else sees it

  • WhatsApp / Meta carries the messages. Meta's own privacy policy applies to what happens inside WhatsApp.
  • Supabase hosts the database where conversations and contact details are stored (data centre region: London, United Kingdom).
  • If automatic AI replies are switched on for a conversation, the text of the message is sent to the AI provider chosen by the project owner so it can write a reply. We turn this on only for testing.

Only the project owner and anyone the owner invites to the workspace can read the conversations.

How long we keep it

Because this is a test project, conversations are kept only while the project runs and are deleted when testing ends or sooner if you ask us to delete them.

Your choices and how to delete your data

You can ask us at any time to show you what we hold about you or to delete it. To do so, email us from the address you use for WhatsApp or tell us the phone number the messages came from, and we will delete the related contact, conversations and messages. We aim to reply within 30 days. You can also simply stop messaging the number.

Children

This project is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
